How it’s calculated

  • Each swing adds the rock’s break % as progress toward 100 (Mithril at 30%: 30, 60, 90, 100 = 4 swings). Swings to break = ceil(100 ÷ break %).
  • Each swing also has that same % chance to break the rock instantly. XP and the main drop are awarded on break.
  • Expected swings per rock = (1 − (1 − p)^H) ÷ p, where p is the break chance and H the swings to break.
  • XP/hr = 2,400 swings × XP per break ÷ expected swings. Breaks/hr = 2,400 ÷ expected swings.
  • Drops/hr = breaks/hr × items per break (1, or 2 for Rune Essence) × (1 + output doubling). Geodes/hr = 2,400 × geode chance × (1 + doubling), since the geode roll is per hit. Only Copper, Iron, Silver, Mithril, Gold and Adamantite show a geode chance. Stone/hr from the Stoner’s Pickaxe = 2,400 × 2.5% × (1 + doubling).
  • Gold/hr = drops × drop price + geodes × geode price + Stone × Stone price. Prices are fixed sell values per item.
  • Shop pickaxe heads add rock break chance: Copper, Steel and Adamant give Ore Veins +25%, +25% and +50%. Iron and Adamant give Essence Veins +25% each. Mithril gives Gem Veins +25%. Bonuses add together.
  • Assumptions: the 0.30% on-hit chance on the rock cards includes the two owned bindings (+0.03% each), so the base is 0.24%. Output doubling applies to every drop. The Stoner’s Pickaxe +2.5% is the value shown while it is damaged.
